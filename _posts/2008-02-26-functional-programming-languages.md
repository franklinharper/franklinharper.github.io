In [Can Your Programming Language Do This?](http://www.joelonsoftware.com/items/2006/08/01.html), Joel Spolsky gives a nice simple explanation on how functional languages can make your code smaller, tighter, more reusable, and more scalable. Google has used this to their advantage in creating [Map Reduce](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MapReduce).
